# Masthead

The nameplate that opens every edition: blackletter title between two ears, then a double rule and a dateline strip.

- `full` sits at the top of the front page, the onboarding welcome and PDF page 1. `compact` is the sticky reader header once the reader scrolls past the full one.
- Provide `date` preformatted ("Saturday, 17 October 2026"), plus any of `edition`, `reader`, `storyCount`, `readingTime`, `location`, `tagline`, `actions`.
- The title is the wordmark: always "The Morrow" in `masthead`, never another face, never recolored from `ink`.
- Personal touches live in the ears ("Prepared for Aadi"), not in the title.
- Under 640px the ears stack beneath the title and the title drops to 44px.
