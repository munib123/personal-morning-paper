# ReadingProgress

- Under the compact masthead in the reader, and inside EditionCards in the archive.
- Provide `value` (0–1) and a `label` in words ("5 of 12 read"). Finishing the paper is the reward: say so ("You finished today's paper") rather than adding confetti or streak badges.
