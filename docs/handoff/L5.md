- `/demo`: signs the seeded persona in (`DEMO_USER_EMAIL` / `DEMO_USER_PASSWORD`) and 307s to `/today` with the `sb-…-auth-token` cookie on the redirect. - `POST /api/onboarding/describe`: `{ text }` (1–1000 chars, else 400) → `{ picks, blocked, provider }`. L2's `mapTextToTopics` runs behind an 8 s timeout and a per-address burst limit (8 model calls a minute, then keywords; it still answers 200). Only slugs in the taxonomy come back. L5's `keywordTopics` answers whenever the model throws, stalls, returns nothing usable, or answers from L2's own keyword matcher (`provider: 'keyword'`); only a real model answer (`provider: 'gemini'`) is passed through.
- `GET /api/taste?picks=a,b,c`: always 200 with 8 cards (6 matched + 2 wildcards at positions 2 and 5), `Cache-Control: private, max-age=300`. Hostile or empty `picks` still get 8 cards.

## Contract requests

1. **For L0 and L3, `OnboardingInput` gaps.** The routine's delivery time (`draft.deliveryHour`: 6, 7 or 8) and step 3's "Sources to avoid" are collected but have no field, so they are not sent. Proposed `chore(contract)`: `deliveryHour?: number` → `profiles.delivery_hour` (default 7), and `avoidSources?: string[]` → `hide_source` rows. Until then every profile gets 7:00.
2. **For L0, Morrow `OnboardingStep` footer.** `.mw-ob__foot` does not wrap, so at 390 px Back + "Skip for now" + "Continue" is about 406 px in 358 px and the page scrolls sideways. Proposed fix in `src/styles/morrow/bundle.css`: `.mw-ob__foot { flex-wrap: wrap; }` and `.mw-ob__right { flex-wrap: wrap; margin-left: auto; }`. L5 works around it by rendering Back as a ghost `Button` above the frame.
3. **For L0, link colours.** `bundle.css` has an unlayered `a { color: inherit; }`. Unlayered rules beat Tailwind's layered utilities, so `text-pen` on an `<a>` has no effect and links render in ink, in every lane. Proposed fix: move the rule into `@layer base` (or write it as `:where(a) { color: inherit; }`). L5 puts the pen colour on an inner `<span>` meanwhile.
4. **For L0, Morrow `Segmented`.** The legend names the `<fieldset>`, but the inner `role="radiogroup"` has no accessible name. Proposed fix: `aria-labelledby` from the radiogroup to the legend.
5. **For L2 (note, not a contract change).** `articles.labels[0]` is "most specific first", not "most confident", and today it is noisy. The preview's kicker shows the `labels[0]` topic, so a sanctions story labelled `sports.cricket.pakistan` (conf 0.42; `sports.other` 0.58) shows as "Pakistan Cricket". Better labels fix it at the source.
6. **For L2, `keywordTopics` in `src/server/enrich/describe.ts`.** Its `NEGATIONS` has no "less" or "fewer", so "more climate tech, less celebrity gossip" returned Pakistani Showbiz as a *pick* (checked against the merged code). The last slug segment is also used as a term, so any "other" in the text picks `sports.other`. L5's describe route therefore passes through only `provider: 'gemini'` answers. Suggested: add `less`/`fewer`, drop one-word generic segments such as `other`, and reuse L5's `keywordTopics` (`@/components/onboarding/lib/keywords`) for "Tell your editor" if that helps.

## For L7 (InterestPicker on `/me`)

- `import InterestPicker from '@/components/onboarding/InterestPicker'` (named export too). Props are exactly §9.6: `{ taxonomy, value: { picks, blocked }, onChange, mode: 'edit' }`. Take the taxonomy from `getTaxonomy()` in `@/server/onboarding`.
- It renders Morrow `TopicChip`s grouped by root, with the › drill-down. `mode="edit"` hides the pick counter. `onChange` fires on every change to picks or blocked.
- The reducer behind it is `pickerReducer` in `@/components/onboarding/lib/picker-state` (`toggle`, `toggleDisclosure`, `block`, `unblock`, `merge`, `set`). Core has no "Not for me" control; a `block` dispatch from your own button works.

## For L3 (`POST /api/onboarding`)

- The body is exactly `OnboardingInput`, validated with `OnboardingInputSchema` before it leaves the browser: `taste: []`, `paperName: 'The Morrow'`, `displayName` trimmed and capped at 24 (or `null`), `city` trimmed and capped at 80 (default Islamabad), `tz` from the browser (default `Asia/Karachi`).
- The response is read with `OnboardingResponseSchema`; anything else shows "Try again".
- A skipped Topics step sends `DEFAULT_PICKS` (`news`, `technical`, `sports`, `business` as tiles), minus any blocked root. Step 3 picks arrive as `via: 'describe'`.
- The guest session comes from `supabase.auth.signInAnonymously()` in the browser; the request carries its cookies.
