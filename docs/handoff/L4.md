- **Front page** (`EditionReader` → `FrontPage`), Morrow only:
  - full masthead ("Edition No. N", "Prepared for {first name}", date · city · N stories · N min read), then a fixed compact masthead with "N of 20 read" once it scrolls away;
  - the editor's note ("Note written with AI" when Gemini wrote it);
  - the 2fr/1fr fold: lead | Morning Recall (L7), "Three things to know", the second story;
  - sections in `sectionOrder`, rows of 3 with column rules, then "Beyond your usual", then "In brief" beside one "Long read".
- **Every story** is a Morrow `StoryCard` fed by `toStoryCardProps`, with:
  - its WhyTag, which opens "Why this story?": N% match, the reasons, one pen bar per score part with the negative part as a subtraction, and More / Less / Hide this source;
  - the source-faithfulness line with its guardrail popover;
  - "Also covered by N outlets", "Read here" (the story sheet) and the FeedbackBar (labelled on the lead, icon-only in narrow columns);
  - a headline link that goes through `/r` and marks the story read.
  - A broken image hides its figure, so the card reads as text-only.
- **Feedback and skip:** More/Less send the signal plus "Got it. More {topic} tomorrow." as a toast and a live-region announcement. Skip removes the card at once and fades a reserve story (same root first) into the slot. Its toast offers Undo and the three reasons for 5 s. The outcome is tracked exactly once: a reason, or `none` on auto-close.
- **Memory:** reads and skips persist per edition in `localStorage` (`subah_edition_{editionId}`, every access guarded). They replay after hydration, so a reload keeps skips and checks.
- **Story sheet** (Radix bottom sheet): it shows the kicker, headline, deck, meta, why, faithfulness line, deep summary, the highlighted key fact, "Read on {domain}" and the FeedbackBar. Opening it marks the story read, logs `open` (sheet) and starts the dwell; closing stops the dwell. Escape closes it and returns focus to "Read here".
- **End of paper:** DoneForToday (L7), then one sentence on tomorrow (biggest weight rise and fall, else this session's More/Less), then Download PDF (L6) and "Past editions". Last comes the closed "How this paper was made" panel: the funnel (articles considered → distinct stories → shortlist → 20 picked + 8 reserve, log scale), build ms, "LLM calls for you", "Ranking is local math, 0 AI calls per reader", the AI vs excerpt share bar with text, and the algorithm version. The masthead link opens the panel.
- **Bytes:** one byte per slide (std summary, no photo) in a scroll-snap deck between the shell bars, under the compact masthead. Each slide has Previous/Next. Keys: j/k next/previous, x skip, o open, s save, b switches view (in both views). Keys are ignored while typing, with Ctrl/Cmd/Alt, or with a dialog open. 8 visible seconds on a slide marks it read and logs `open` (bytes) once. The last slide is the end of the paper.
- **`/r/[articleId]`:**
  - 302 to the article's http(s) URL;
  - `read_original` for the signed-in reader (`src=web`), `qr_scan` for the edition owner (`src=print&e=`), both through `processSignals` inside `after()`;
  - 404 for a non-uuid id, an unknown id, or a `javascript:`/`data:` URL;
  - preview fixture ids redirect without logging.
- **`useSignals`** (same §9.4 signature) wraps the exported `createSignalQueue`:
  - 2 s batches of ≤ 50, capped at 200 queued;
  - keepalive when the tab hides, `sendBeacon` on pagehide;
  - every valid `EventsResultSchema` result sets `lastResult` and calls `publishStreak(result.streak)`.
- **Runs on:** `/today/preview` uses the fixture edition. `/today` and `/paper/[date]` use whatever `buildEdition`/`getEdition` return (L3's stubs until L3 merges).

## Signals

L4 emits exactly the §9.7 fields:

| Type | Fields |
|---|---|
| `impression` | `articleId`, `meta.slot` (via `observeImpression` on each story's `data-slot` wrapper, ≥ 1 s at 50%, once) |
| `open` | `articleId`, `meta.mode: 'sheet'` (sheet opened) or `'bytes'` (8 visible seconds) |
| `dwell` | `articleId`, `value` ms (paused while hidden), `meta.mode: 'sheet' \| 'bytes'` |
| `read_original` / `qr_scan` | `articleId`, `meta.src: 'web' \| 'print'`, plus `editionId` when valid (logged by `/r` through `processSignals`) |
| `skip` | `articleId`, `meta.reason: 'none' \| 'not_interested' \| 'already_seen' \| 'not_today'` |
| `more` / `less` / `save` | `articleId` |
| `hide_source` | `articleId`, `meta.sourceId` |

The queue stamps `editionId` on every event. `useSignals` calls `publishStreak(result.streak)` after every flush that returns a valid result. `startDwell(articleId, mode = 'sheet')` keeps the optional `mode`.

## Contract notes

- **Reserve promotion:** the first reserve story with the same root as the skipped one, else `reserve[0]`. The promoted copy takes the slot's `rank`, `slot` and `tier` (a lead is always `deep`) and keeps its own `sectionSlug`.
  - Undo re-inserts by rank.
  - Skipping a story that was itself promoted finalizes the earlier skip.
  - With the reserve empty the card goes and the total drops ("7 of 19 read").
- **Storage:** `localStorage` key `subah_edition_{editionId}` holds `{ read, skipped }`. Garbage or blocked storage gives a fresh edition.
- **% match:** `round(clamp(total, 0, 1) × 100)`.
- **Shortlist:** 200 = `RankParams.shortlist` unless the receipt carries one (contract request 3).
- **Server toasts:** "Got it…" toasts from `/api/events` are not shown, because the client note already acknowledged More/Less. Other toasts show once.
- **Morrow facts:**
  - `StoryCard` wraps `why` in its own `WhyTag`, so `WhyChip` renders only the content (the sheet wraps it itself).
  - The headline link goes through `/r` and `onOpen` marks the story read.
  - Morrow's `<img>` has no `onError`/`referrerPolicy`, so L4 uses a capture-phase fallback.
  - No `PullQuote` is rendered because `ArticleCard` has no quote field.
- **End of paper:** L7's `DoneForToday` renders "That's your paper for today." and the quiet streak line, so L4 renders neither again. L4 adds no second `StreakChip` either: the shell's TopBar already has one.
- No §9.6 stub mismatch was found.
