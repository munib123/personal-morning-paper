### Layout as built (measured on A4, see Rulings in the plan ledger)

- **Page 1:** Morrow `Masthead` (nameplate 60 px, ears "Edition No. N" / "Prepared for {name}", strip date · city · "Page 1 of N"); lead in columns 1–3 (`print-lead`, deck, picture, 2-column body with a drop cap, source + QR); columns 4–5: `TakeawayBox` "Three things to know", **one** rail story (its QR beside the text), the Inside index; heavy rule; 5 teasers that link to their page.
- **Inner pages:** running head (Morrow compact masthead), section head, the section lead opened with `print-lead`, picture and `PullQuote`, every story in 3–5 ruled columns; a page with 3 stories or fewer also carries an "In brief" strip.
- **Last page:** "Beyond your usual" (reason in pen) and any remaining briefs in columns 1–3; "Why these stories" (before/after bars, reason counts) and `TakeawayBox` "Three things you learned" in 4–5.
- **Every page:** footer "Prepared for {name} · from N sources" in the bottom margin.
- **Overflow guard:** measures every sheet; page 1 → lead 120 words → rail story to briefs → lead 60 words; inner page → its brief strip back to the last page → its last story; last page → briefs 30 words → drop briefs → drop Beyond stories. Up to 4 re-layouts; anything left is logged as `[pdf] pages still overflow after the guard`.

## Env needed

- `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` (admin client: Storage + `editions.pdf_path`).
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`: baked into every QR code and link at render time. All committed and stored PDFs were rendered with `http://localhost:3000`. **Re-render once the deploy URL is known** (commands below).
- `PUPPETEER_EXECUTABLE_PATH`: system Chrome for local renders (installs use `PUPPETEER_SKIP_DOWNLOAD=true`). In Actions, either set it or let Puppeteer download Chrome.
- `DEMO_MODE=1`: lets `/paper/<date>/print?fixture=1` show the fixture in production.
- Storage: the private bucket `papers` comes from L0's migration (verified: exists, `public=false`); `ensurePapersBucket` only recreates it if missing. `pdf_path` = `{userId}/{date}-{hash}.pdf` (no bucket prefix).

## Known risks

- **In-route render:** inside a Next route handler the Morrow components are client references, so the on-miss render throws "Attempted to call Masthead() from the server" and the route answers 503 → print route (logged as `[pdf] render failed`). Locally this costs 2–8 s before the fallback. Normal path: PDFs pre-rendered by the job or `render-pdfs.ts`. On Vercel the route never tries (immediate 503).
- A fresh reader onboarded after the last pre-render gets the print route until `render-pdfs.ts --all-missing` (or the daily job) runs.
- QR codes and links point at whatever `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL` was at render time (currently `http://localhost:3000`).
- Renders read `src/styles/morrow/*.css` and `src/app/fonts/*.woff2` from `process.cwd()`: run them from the repo root.
- Chrome on Windows does not hyphenate (no hyphenation dictionary in a fresh profile), so justified columns can show wide word gaps, most visibly next to the drop cap.
- Very long extractive copy is trimmed by the guard (shorter lead, briefs moved or dropped, at worst a Beyond story dropped). The log line names any page that still overflows.
