## Done

Everything below runs on **real data** in the shared database (1,805 articles from L1's ingest).

- **`@/server/nlp`:** `embedTexts` and `runNlp` (raw → embedded: labels, clusters, importance).
  - Embeddings: local `Supabase/gte-small` (384-d, normalized). `Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` is the fallback if gte-small fails to load. `EMBEDDINGS_PROVIDER=gemini` switches to `gemini-embedding-2` at 384 dims, with one `retryDelay` wait on a 429.
  - All 1,805 articles carry `embed_model = local:Supabase/gte-small`, so there is one vector space. All 67 topics are embedded (`scripts/embed-topics.ts`).
  - **Clusters, tuned on the real corpus:** the threshold is **0.90** (the spec started at 0.82).
    - Why: gte-small's cosines are compressed. At 0.82, unrelated stories chained into 117 clusters of 12.
    - Evidence: across 1.3 M same-window pairs, 0.80–0.88 is mostly "different story, same topic", and 0.90+ is mostly the same story.
    - Result: 1,338 clusters, 137 with 2+ sources, all same-story on inspection. Example: the Tesla flying Roadster has 12 articles from 10 sources, importance 1.0.
  - **Labels:** the search goes global when the best prior-subtree topic trails the global best by ≥ 0.03, because the 0.30 floor never fires with gte-small. Before this, world news in a sports feed was labelled Pakistan Cricket. `cli.ts relabel` updated 402 non-Gemini rows.
- **`@/server/enrich`:**
  - `runEnrich`: cluster representatives, ≥ 3 per level-2 topic, cap 400, batches of 10, per-article `llm_cache`, strict guardrails, one re-batch, then extractive.
  - `writeEditorNote` in the Morrow voice: SMART for demo personas, FAST otherwise, with the template as the fallback.
  - `mapTextToTopics`: **Gemini first** (stretch S1, done). It runs on the FAST ladder with a 6 s deadline for the whole ladder and keeps only valid slugs (picks ≤ 8, blocked ≤ 5). The keyword matcher (with negation) is its fallback. It never throws.
    - Live: "cricket, AI and a bit of space, no showbiz" → picks cricket, ai-ml, space; blocked pakistani-showbiz; `provider: 'gemini'`; 1.8 s.
  - Also exported: `templateNote` and `keywordTopics`.
  - **Gemini is sent only articles with 120+ words.** In the first run, articles of 40–80 words were enriched 11% of the time and 120+ words 62–83%. Shorter texts stay extractive, so they are still rankable.
  - **The instructions keep features, guides and tutorials usable.** The first run lost 29 long dev tutorials as "not news". `cli.ts revive-unusable` returned those rows to extractive stock.
  - **Corpus after the third run: 612 `enriched` (`gemini:gemini-3.5-flash-lite`), 1,330 `fallback` (extractive, whole sentences), 22 `unusable`.** Every story that has a representative of 120+ words now has a Gemini article.
    - Every enriched row has a camelCase `recall` and a `key_fact`.
    - 0 Gemini headlines contain `!` or `?`. 27 enriched rows use the publisher's title as their headline.
- **`@/server/ai`:** `callLLM`, `geminiStatus()` and `getLlmCallCount()`.
  - `callLLM` walks the ladder SMART → FAST → FALLBACK → fallback. It waits the 429 `retryDelay` once, has a 60 s breaker, skips a model on a per-day 429 until 00:00 PT, paces each model by its own RPM, and keeps the `llm_usage` budget guard (atomic `increment_llm_usage` RPC) and `llm_cache`.
  - Reads and writes to `llm_usage`, `llm_cache` and `topics` time out after 2.5–5 s, so a slow database never stalls a request (`/api/health`, describe).
  - Live today: 160 FAST requests (of 450) and 1 SMART, 0 rate-limited.
  - When one model's pacing slot falls after the deadline, only that model is skipped. Before this fix, a third back-to-back SMART note went straight to the template even though FAST was free.

## Notes for other lanes

- **L3:**
  - Labels are most-specific-first `{slug, conf}`; conf sums to 1.
  - `importance` ∈ [0,1], and it is 0 when no cluster has 2+ sources. `clusters.source_count` is current.
  - `enrich_provider` `gemini:*` maps to `aiProvider 'gemini'`. Enriched rows have `recall` stored camelCase `{question, options, answerIndex, explanation}` (add `articleId`) and a `key_fact`. Extractive rows have `recall = null` and `why_it_matters = null`.
  - Gemini headlines are in the Morrow voice.
  - `writeEditorNote` never throws. Pass `paperName: BRAND.defaultPaperName()` and `preferSmart: profile.isDemo`.
  - `getLlmCallCount()` is there for `receipt.llmCallsThisEdition`.
- **Ops / job:ingest:**
  - `runNlp` then `runEnrich` (cap 400) are live. Each run costs about 40–60 FAST requests, and only while unenriched stories remain: clusters that already have a Gemini article are skipped.
  - The FAST budget today is 160 of 450.
- **L7, wave 2 ("Tell your editor"):** `mapTextToTopics(text)` is the hook. In wave 1 it is keyword-only; the signature stays when S1 adds Gemini.
- **L1:** the `the-news-international-sports` feed carries general world news. The label rule now corrects most of these, but the feed URL is worth checking.
- **Known limits:**
  - Articles under 120 words stay extractive.
  - Non-representative cluster members stay extractive.
  - Across concurrent processes, the RPD guard can overshoot by the requests already in flight.
