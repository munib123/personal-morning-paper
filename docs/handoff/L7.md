### Streak engine (`src/server/streaks/`)
- `computeStreak(days, today, localHour = 12)` (`compute.ts`) is pure and replays every `daily_activity` row on each call, so `streaks` is only a rebuildable cache.
  - **Freezes and repair (stretch Task 15, landed):** a freeze is earned every 7 streak days (start 1, hold at most 2) and is applied automatically to a missed day (the run holds, +0). With no freeze left, exactly one missed day within 48 h is `repairable`: answering a recall (`recall_answer`) marks it `repaired` (+1 to the run, not to `totalDays`), at most once per 14 days. A second miss breaks the streak. `longest` and `totalDays` only grow; frozen and repaired days never count toward `totalDays`.
  - States: `new` → `repairable` → `done_today` → (`at_risk` at ≥ 20:00 **or 00:00–02:59** local, else `frozen` when yesterday was covered by a freeze, else `pending`) → `broken`. Milestones 3/7/14/30/50/100 only on `done_today`.
  - Replay decisions: one row per date (max of the counts, OR of `editionComplete`); rows dated after `today` are ignored (the demo clock moved back); `last7` marks today unread as `today`, then `repaired`, `frozen` (stored row or auto-applied), `read` or `missed`. Auto-applied freezes are persisted as `frozen` rows (never overwriting a stored day) by both `recordActivity` and `getStreak`.
- QR rules (`activity.ts`, §9.7): an `open` plus a `dwell` on the same article with `value ≥ 20000` (`meta.mode` sheet or missing) or `≥ 8000` (bytes); or `read_original`, `qr_scan`, `recall_answer`. Counts are distinct per article per day and dwells are max-not-sum, so replayed batches (sendBeacon retries, refreshes) never inflate a day. `storiesRead` counts opens, long dwells, read_original and qr_scan.
- `recordActivity(userId, events, now, tz)` unions the day's stored `events` with the batch (works whether L3 inserts before or after calling it), upserts `daily_activity` and the `streaks` row, and returns the view with `milestone` set **only on the call that made today a streak day** (a read, or a repair later in the day). Edition complete = ≥ 5 stories read **and** recall answered; a day with no recall questions counts as answered. A batch with no reading signal (impressions only) writes nothing. **It never throws**: any storage error logs and returns a neutral `new` view (`FIXTURE_STREAK` with `DEMO_FALLBACK=1`).
- `getStreak` (milestone always null) and `getHeatmap` (N days, 5 levels by stories read). `clock.ts`: `localHour(now, tz)`, `nextMorningIso(now, tz)` (next edition day at 07:00 local, as UTC ISO; DST-safe, half-hour zones tested).
- `repo.ts`: `StreakRepo` with `supabaseStreakRepo()` (admin client; newest 400 days; `upsertStatusDays` for frozen/repaired days; the `streaks` row stores `freezes_available`, `freeze_progress` and, when a repair happens, `last_repair_at`) and `memoryStreakRepo()` (tests).
- **Verified on the shared Supabase** with a throwaway auth user created and deleted by a script: 3 days of reads → `done_today` 3 with milestone 3 only on the first call of the day, `daily_activity` and `streaks` rows written (`freezes_available` 1, `freeze_progress` 3), two days later `getStreak` → `frozen` with a persisted `frozen` row, heatmap 84 days; the user's rows cascaded away on delete.
