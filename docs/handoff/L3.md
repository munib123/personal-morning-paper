## Notes for other lanes

- `WeightDelta.name` is filled by `withNames` after `applySignal`; rank-v1 constants outside `RankParams` live in `RANK_V1_FIXED` (`src/server/rank/params.ts`).
- The `topic` chip follows the Morrow voice: `Because you follow {name}` (picked node or ancestor; names the picked node) / `You read {n} stories on {name} this week` (via `rankWithCounts` + `topicReadCounts`) / `More from {name}`. Explore and serendipity stories carry their slot chip only.
- `Edition.sectionOrder` = roots of the core-slot stories ordered by Σ score (same as `FIXTURE_EDITION`).
